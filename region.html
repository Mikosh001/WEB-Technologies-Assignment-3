About Mangystau

A region of Caspian views and distinctive rock formations, presented through places that matter to me.

The coast

Aktau and the Caspian

Aktau provides the coastal side of this guide: sea views, a waterfront promenade, and a place to begin exploring Mangystau.

I chose Mangystau because it is my home region. The photographs here show real places rather than generic scenery.
